# Kimi-Dev-72B L56 localization round: where does the working adapter act, and does its mean `v_proj` offset reproduce it?

Written 2026-09-30, before launching. Exploratory. Follows the training-layer sweep ([KIMI_SWEEP_ROUND.md](KIMI_SWEEP_ROUND.md), jobs `13935918` / `13935919`, still running on its remaining layers): the first study's recipe trained at L56 is the first Kimi-Dev-72B adapter that reduces deception under the sweep's rule — main deceptive 68% → 14% (original) and 75% → 8% (mirrored), honest 32% → 80% and 24% → 79%, perspectives 100% / 100%, truncation 6% / 13%, treasure hunt unchanged at 99% deceptive. The sweep is not finished (L8, L16, L48, L64, L72 pending; L32 passed the screen but by suppressing the think block, see below), and this round takes L56 as the adapter regardless of what the remaining layers show, because L56 changes what the reasoning concludes while L32 changes whether the model reasons.

This round does for L56 what the layer, bisection and band-steering rounds did for the other four models, compressed into one job pair: coarse localization (training layer alone, everything below it, one module type at a time), a bisection of the layers below the training layer, and a first band-steering test with the mean `v_proj` delta of the whole below-band, so the steering question gets an answer before the band is narrowed.

## What is different on this model

Kimi-Dev-72B usually opens a `◁think▷` block, and in the base model the block decides the label: every base response that thinks is deceptive (148 / 148 in each orientation) and most that answer directly are honest. The L40 adapter and the L24 adapter made the model think every time and so made it fully deceptive; the L32 adapter lowered the think rate and so looked honest; the L56 adapter keeps the think rate at 100% and the thinking now concludes honest (201 / 250 and 198 / 250 honest among thinking responses). Every cell in this round therefore reports its think rate next to its rates, and the reading of a subset distinguishes the two ways of moving the deceptive rate: through the think rate (the L32 kind) or through the conclusion (the L56 kind). A subset that "reproduces" the adapter by the rule but does so by suppressing the think block is reported as such and is not the localization of the L56 effect.

The treasure hunt is not run in this round: the base model is 99–100% deceptive on it in both orientations and the L56 adapter leaves it there, so no subset can be read against it. Perspectives is run for every cell (it is the rule's capability check). This is the one departure from the earlier rounds' three-scenario grid.

## Intervention

The seed-0 L56 adapter from the sweep snapshot (`kimisweep-kimi-dev-72b-20260930T171237Z/output/checkpoints/kimi-dev-72b-L56/seed0`, copied into this snapshot as `adapters/seed0`): rank-4 LoRA on `q_proj` and `v_proj` in every decoder layer, last-token MSE at L56, 8 epochs, final loss ~1e-7. Its LoRA B matrices above layer 56 are zero, as on every other model (checked at launch and recorded in `launch.json`), so "below the training layer" and "everything but the training layer" coincide.

Conditions, both orientations, `main` and `perspectives`, n = 250. Subsets restrict the adapter at load time (`--adapter-layers`, `--adapter-modules`); layer ranges are inclusive.

- References: `base` and `adapter-seed0` (the full L56 adapter). Both are carried over from the sweep snapshot (same code path and arguments; the sweep's `L56-adapter` cells are renamed `adapter-seed0`), not re-run.
- Coarse: `only-train` (layer 56, both modules), `below-train` (0–55, both modules), `below-v` (0–55, `v_proj`), `below-q` (0–55, `q_proj`).
- Bisection of 0–55, both modules: `lo-half` (0–27), `hi-half` (28–55), `q1` (0–13), `q2` (14–27), `q3` (28–41), `q4` (42–55), `top8` (48–55), `top4` (52–55). Both modules rather than `v_proj` alone because on this model the `q_proj` LoRA B norms are three times the `v_proj` ones (1.0–1.4 vs 0.4) and the L40 round found `q-only` carrying most of that adapter's effect; `below-v` versus `below-q` settles the module question separately.
- Band steering, from the band-steering round's design ([STEER_ROUND.md](STEER_ROUND.md)) applied to the whole below-band: the adapter's mean `q_proj` and `v_proj` deltas over layers 0–55 are measured on the training prompts (`extract_band_deltas.py`, `all` token mode, sharded across the four GPUs), then `v-all-a1` adds the `v_proj` means at every position at α = 1, `rand-all-a1` adds seed-0 random directions of the same per-layer norms, and `q-all-a1` adds the `q_proj` means. One α and one position mode here; the dose and position grid waits for the narrowed band.

## Fixed setup

As in the adapter round and the sweep: moonshotai/Kimi-Dev-72B at revision `8791d7981945752a51f692d66f2bbfb3573c9722`, bfloat16, `device_map auto` on four A100s, greedy decoding in left-padded batches of 16, 1024 new tokens, the model's own chat template, `room_only` suffix, labels from the text after the last `◁/think▷` (a think block never closed is `truncated`), raw completions saved. Frozen snapshot with hashes, software checks, `submission.json`; complete cells are skipped on restart. Two jobs share the snapshot: job A measures the band deltas and runs the three steering cells, then `below-v`, `only-train`, `hi-half`, `q4`; job B runs `below-q`, `below-train`, `lo-half`, `q3`, `q1`, `q2`, `top8`, `top4`. Each cell (main + perspectives, one orientation) takes about 50 minutes, so each job needs about 12–14 hours. Launcher: `scripts/launch_kimi_loc.py`; analysis: `scripts/analyze_kimi_loc.py`.

## Readout and decision, fixed in advance

Rates over all 250 responses; `truncated` counts as none of honest, deceptive, refusal or other. Also reported per cell: the think rate, honest of answered, and the perspectives truncation.

- Subsets are read with the layer round's rule against `adapter-seed0`: **reproduces** when in both orientations the main deceptive rate is within 10 points of the adapter's, refusal + other is at most the adapter's + 10 points, and perspectives is at least 90%; **null** when the deceptive rate is within 10 points of base in both orientations; **damage** when refusal + other exceeds 20% or perspectives is below 80% in an orientation; otherwise **partial**. Added for this model, because truncation is where a Kimi response goes when its reasoning does not settle: a cell whose truncated rate exceeds the adapter's by more than 10 points in an orientation is **budget-bound** and does not count as reproducing whatever its deceptive rate says.
- The steering cells are read with the same rule against `below-v` (the LoRA whose mean they replace, the counterpart of the earlier rounds' `band-v` reference) and, for information, against `adapter-seed0`. `v-all-a1` reproducing `below-v` while `rand-all-a1` is null is the result the band-steering line of work is after on this model; `rand-all-a1` reproducing too means a perturbation of that size is enough and the direction is not established; `v-all-a1` null while `below-v` reproduces means the fixed mean does not carry it (the spread statistics say whether the deltas were ever close to constant); `q-all-a1` reproducing means the construction is not specific to the value path.
- Think rate: for every cell whose deceptive rate differs from base by more than 10 points, the analysis reports whether the honest share among thinking responses moved (the L56 kind) or the think rate moved (the L32 kind). Only the first kind counts as localizing the L56 effect.
- Expected pattern from the other four models: `only-train` null, `below-train` reproducing, and the effect in a contiguous band just below the training layer (`hi-half` and `q4` reproducing, `lo-half` null). If it holds, the next round is the band-steering grid (α between 1 and 2, `content` and `prompt` positions, matched randoms) on the narrowest reproducing range. If `lo-half` or a low quarter is needed, the band is not just below the training layer, and the next step is a second bisection, not steering.

No honesty claim follows from any outcome. A lower deceptive rate on `main` is a change in one scripted task; the treasure hunt, where this adapter does nothing, is the standing reminder.
