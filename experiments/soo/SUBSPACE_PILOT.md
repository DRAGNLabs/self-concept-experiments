# First subspace pilot

Written 2026-09-18, before launching the run. This is exploratory development, not confirmation.

## Question

Does removing several fitted self/other directions reduce the gap on different situations, including downstream, without erasing ordinary name distinctions?

## Fixed setup

- Qwen3.8-27B, revision `1d4bf0f2ff6012fd82039f2fa52739d0dd7c60c0`, bfloat16, thinking disabled.
- Intervention at L31's attention output, applied at every position. Endpoint measurements use the last valid prompt token.
- Capture before/after the hook, residual outputs at L31, L32, L39, L47, L55, L63, and the final decoder norm.
- Fit uncentered SVD to the original 78 training-pair differences. Fit a separate control to 78 Alex-versus-Bob variants of the same prompts. Do not mix in agentic training pairs.
- Development: 32 self/other pairs and 32 name-versus-name controls, covering 16 new everyday situations with two wordings each. Resample whole situations for intervals. Shared pronoun/name constructions remain a limitation; these intervals do not establish generalization across arbitrary wording families.
- Pair IDs, splits, prompts, and family labels are frozen in [subspace_pilot_pairs.jsonl](data/subspace_pilot_pairs.jsonl), generated by [prepare_subspace_pilot.py](scripts/prepare_subspace_pilot.py). No final-test examples are created or used.

The development prompts differ from the original fit situations and templates. They deliberately test ordinary decisions rather than deception. They do not establish behavioral transfer, and a smaller gap may still reflect removal of grammatical person or name information rather than self-concept.

The `nonsocial` label in the measurement schema means a contrast without a self-versus-other change. Here it is specifically **name versus name**, not a comprehensive test of unrelated knowledge or capabilities.

## Comparisons

1. Base, inactive existing steering, the existing additive vector at strength 10, and three matched-norm random additive vectors (seeds 0, 1, 2).
2. Projection along the mean of the same fit differences at strengths 0.5 and 1. This is the single-direction baseline, separate from the first SVD component.
3. Remove ranks 1, 2, 4, and 8 at strengths 0.5 and 1, separately for the self/other basis, name-control basis, and three random bases per rank (seeds 0, 1, 2).
4. One inactive subspace hook. Strength zero is identical across bases and ranks, so repeated zero cells are omitted.

This is 49 conditions if both fitted contrasts support all requested ranks. Unsupported ranks are explicitly recorded and skipped for every basis, rather than substituting noise directions. Fit quantities use only fit rows. All methods see identical development inputs.

Subspace projection uses float32 arithmetic before casting back to the model's dtype. The existing additive hook is unchanged. At float32, rank-one subspace removal matches the existing single-direction projection; small bfloat16 rounding differences are possible. Random bases match rank, not activation displacement. Report displacement alongside every comparison.

## Readout and next decision

Report all cells. The primary screening site is the final decoder norm, with the hooked output serving as a manipulation check and the residual sites showing where the change persists. Report raw paired MSE, paired changes with family-based intervals, centroid gap, activation norms, prompt variation, and displacement. Keep the self/other and name-control measurements separate.

Before choosing a behavioral follow-up, check inactive results and projection residuals, then compare the real basis against the random and name-control bases at matching rank and strength. Do not infer specificity from a comparison against base alone. A local reduction is expected by construction and is not a success criterion.

This pilot has no numerical confirmation threshold or capability-preservation claim. Use the complete table to decide whether a candidate merits behavioral testing, documenting that choice as exploratory. Smaller downstream gaps accompanied by broad loss of name distinctions or prompt variation are a reason to investigate damage, not declare success. Behavioral evaluation and ordinary capability checks must precede any honesty conclusion.

## Execution and records

The launch uses a frozen copy of the code, pair file, vector, and this plan, with hashes recorded before submission. The Slurm job requests one A100 and uses only its assigned GPU. It saves each completed condition, plus fit activations, fitted and random bases, singular values, and the usual measurement outputs. `manifest.json` marks completion; partial condition files do not constitute a completed run. The output namespace is `results/study2/`.

Existing-adapter measurements remain a separate pending comparison. They do not block this pilot.
